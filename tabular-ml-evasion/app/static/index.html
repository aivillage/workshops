Why this matters

    

      Automated systems increasingly decide financial access, fraud status,
      insurance eligibility, and more.  
      These systems often operate as black boxes.
      


      When decisions affect people’s lives, who has agency?
      The system? The company? The attacker? The individual?
      


      This challenge lets you explore how fragile automated decision systems can be —
      and how limited feedback can still reveal their internal logic.
